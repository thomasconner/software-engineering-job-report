## Decisions made autonomously

These were reported to Thomas, and he can change them.

- **Linear:** project P-CON-53 in the Conner Technology team.
- **Models:**
  - No Haiku. Regex handles salary and company names, and Jev is the only model.
  - Jev is pinned to `jev-1.14.0`.
- **Data rules:**
  - Backfill from 2011-04, using a 7-day snapshot window.
  - Count submitted posts rather than live ones.
- **Validation:** labels come from AI annotators, which is disclosed on the page; human labels are pending.
- **Seekers:** job-seeker posts are never quoted. Samples are hiring posts only.
- **Pipeline:**
  - Classify only complete months.
  - CI classifies at most 3 months per run.
  - Answers are committed as Parquet, and the Jev cache is gitignored.
- **First issue:** October 2026, because November wasn't complete yet.
- **October correction:** published on 2026-11-04 without a PR, since Thomas had approved the correction. October stays archived in its original HN-themed framing.
- **Noise bars:**
  - Each series' bars are estimated from its own history.
  - Salary bars are bootstrap-only.
  - Mood bars have a 0.2 floor.
- **Outside data:**
  - Indeed comes from GitHub, not FRED, dated by its first-parent commits.
  - JOLTS uses the unadjusted series, drawn as a 12-month average.
- **Job-board panel:**
  - No big tech; the SEC panel covers it.
  - pear-vc is flagged as a portfolio board.
  - Western Digital is swapped for Asana on 2026-12-01.
- **Brand and URL:** the wordmark is now "Software Engineering Jobs Report". The repo name and URL are unchanged.
